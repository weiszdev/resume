## Specialization: Complex Chronic Illness & Root-Cause Recovery

I apply the same root-cause engineering discipline I used to train 100+ mechanical engineers — diagnose, isolate, rebuild — to complex, hard-to-diagnose chronic illness. My focus is tick-borne illness and co-infections (Lyme and related infections), mold-related illness (CIRS), post-viral syndromes, and post-concussive/TBI recovery: conditions where standard workups routinely come back "normal" despite ongoing symptoms. This specialization is grounded in my own multi-year recovery from a complex, multi-system chronic illness, in addition to seven years of functional medicine study.

- **Founder & Program Developer, *The Root Cause Reset*** — a structured 12-week Diagnose → Isolate → Rebuild coaching program for complex chronic illness, delivered through NotDrWise.com
- Personal recovery from a multi-system chronic illness (tick-borne co-infections, mold exposure, and traumatic brain injury) informs a client-facing, lived-experience approach to root-cause coaching
- Functional lab literacy across tick-borne/co-infection panels, methylation, and adrenal function, applied to translate complex results into a clear recovery plan
